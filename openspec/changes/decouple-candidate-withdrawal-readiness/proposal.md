# Proposal: decouple candidate withdrawal from readiness

## Why

The Human has decided that the unmerged candidate for #478 must be withdrawn, while its requirements and immutable Change survive and a later candidate is rebuilt from fresh main. Withdrawal must not wait for the new proposal or implementation to be ready. Current main still couples accepted PR retirement to Lead / resolve-question / ready and an implement-change successor. The same restriction appears in accepted-intent retention, exact target qualification, close-only cardinality, and later carrier proof. A truthful research-required result therefore cannot yet carry the same exact accepted close obligation.

The preceding helper qualify-accepted-carrier-source has completed its implementation and archive lifecycle. At this proposal's observed N-1 revision f327e5f7f054affa40120c8ca93a2804032a3f25, the shared carrier reader supports qualified raw and legacy accepted originals. This proposal builds on that deployed source; it does not repeat that helper or inherit any acceptance from the abandoned parent candidate.

This increment also resolves directly relevant canonical wording that still describes Explore as exclusively pre-Change or maps Human-decision results to an obsolete Explore continuation. The current executable Action model remains the sole routing owner. The correction distinguishes initial intake from bounded research of an already activated immutable Change.

## What Changes

- Permit one exact, independently authorized candidate withdrawal while the mapped Lead result truthfully describes the remaining candidate work.
- Keep the effect close-only, preserve one accepted obligation, and reconstruct its exact raw or legacy source and causal completion across interruption.
- Establish a same-Issue, same-Change, no-replacement research frontier without forcing READY, creating a replacement, or resetting Change to unset.
- Consolidate capability, intent retention, cardinality, carrier proof, and reconciliation around their existing executable owners.
- Preserve generic PR mutation boundaries: new Explore or Propose coverage permits no ordinary title/body/base/reopen/ref or replacement mutation.
- Clarify the Lead role and the two materially affected action Skills; preserve independent review, Human freshness, current dispatch, exact head, and native carrier boundaries.
