# Why

An approved separate follow-up can reach a readiness boundary without an executable tracker or its necessary dependencies. In #470 the accepted correction materialized Change documents, and the later review found the missing tracker. The earlier review preceded the new Human input. The Human decision recorded in [#474](https://github.com/royhsu-work/investment-strategy/issues/474#issuecomment-7152140804) permits transient duplicate physical Issues and preserves the original delivery obligations.

# What Changes

- Complete each qualified required defer decision through the existing Application. Persist its source, verify a queueable tracker or qualified successor state, and observe the necessary Issue dependencies before releasing the affected boundary. Omitted worker effects cannot waive required work.
- Reuse a qualified tracker whenever possible. A new, fully observed and reauthorized create is legal after an interrupted or uncertain POST. Several legitimate trackers may coexist. Independent prerequisites and admission rules apply to every carrier.
- Reconcile dependency declarations in the existing Issue DAG. Preserve independent edges during concurrent complete-set corrections. Keep pending materialization recoverable even when its own consequence suspends the producer.
- Revalidate the original scope against deployed main at Explore or a material resume boundary. Retire unnecessary work through existing Lead and Application ownership, including exact PR disposition, independent accepted effects and cleanup.

## Scope and non-goals

Deliver one Change on current N-1. Preserve Formal-first, WIP=1, one Action per wake, independent review, merge and archive gates. Maintain existing issue dependencies and approved scopes. No obligation IDs, tracker election, tracker ownership, cross-Issue completion mapping, parallel graph, semantic equivalence classifier, new Action, scheduler, lock, cursor or generic write retry. No additional follow-up is required.

## Exit

Verify the normal Application and Dispatch entry points across interruption and current-main revalidation, including absence of a failure artifact. Verify exact-source adoption, route and dependency read-back, race-safe independent corrections, partial scope, terminal cleanup and eventual recovery on a legal wake. Land only through the existing independent review and lifecycle.
